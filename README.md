### **Detection of Manipulative and Gaslighting Patterns in Text Using NLP**

**Overview**

Manipulative and gaslighting language is a form of psychological abuse where a person uses words to confuse, control, or make someone question their own thoughts, feelings, or memories. Detecting such language is challenging because it is often subtle, context-dependent, and does not always contain obvious negative or abusive words.

Traditional Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques mainly rely on keyword matching or sentiment analysis. While these methods can identify explicit hate speech or offensive language, they often fail to recognize manipulation because the harmful intent is hidden within the conversation and depends on context rather than individual words.

This project aims to develop an NLP-based system that can automatically detect manipulative and gaslighting patterns in text by analyzing conversational context, linguistic cues, intent, and psychological manipulation strategies. Using modern deep learning models such as Transformer-based architectures (e.g., BERT, RoBERTa, or DistilBERT), the system can understand the meaning of sentences in context instead of relying only on keywords.

The proposed model processes conversations, extracts semantic features, and classifies text into categories such as **Manipulative**, **Gaslighting**, or **Non-manipulative**. This can help identify emotionally abusive communication in social media, messaging platforms, emails, online forums, and customer interactions.

The system has potential applications in mental health support, online safety, cyberbullying prevention, content moderation, and relationship counseling. By providing early detection of manipulative communication, it can assist users, moderators, and professionals in recognizing harmful interactions and promoting healthier online conversations.
